Air Fryer Pork Chops

Description

These breaded pork chops made in air fryer.

Ingredients

  • 4 (5 ounce) boneless, center-cut prok chops, 1-inch think
  • 1 teaspoon Cajun seasoning
  • 1 1/2 cups cheese and garlic croutons
  • 2 large eggs
  • cooking spray

Directions

  1. Preheat and air fryer to 400 degrees
  2. Place pork chopson a plate and season both sides with Cajun
  3. Pulse Croutons in an small food processor until fine
  4. Place 2 chops in the basket of the air fryer and cook for 10 minutes
  5. Serve and enjoy
