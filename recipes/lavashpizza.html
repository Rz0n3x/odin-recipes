Lavash Pizza recipe

Lavash Pizza is a quick and easy appetizer.

Ingredients

  • 1 package lavash flatbread
  • 1/2 cup pizza souce
  • 8 ounces mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 2 ounces pepperoni, or as need

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 330 degrees. Spray one side of lavash with nonstick spray.
  2. Bake Lavash directly on the rack in the preheated oven for 2 minutes
  3. Carefully remove lavash from oven, place on a baking tray.
  4. Sprinkle 2 onuces shredded mozzarella cheese on each lavash. Top with pepperoni
  5. Bake pizza until cheese is melted and golden, about 20 minutes
